# Research working notes -- unvetted

**Nothing in this directory is a finding.** These are raw evidence bases assembled during research: fetched URLs, extracted quotations, and gap lists. They are published so that a reader or a continuing author can see what was fetched before it was filtered, and so that continuation does not require re-fetching everything.

They have **not** been through the citation audit that `../falsifiers/adjudications/CITATION-AUDIT-2026-08-19.md` applied to the determinations. Specifically:

- Quotations here have not all been re-verified against the source page.
- Some entries record claims the adjudications went on to **reject**. Presence here is not endorsement.
- Source standing is not consistently labelled. The determinations label it; these notes often do not.
- The gap sections list what was sought and not found. Those gaps are real and were load-bearing in several determinations.

**Where a note here conflicts with a published determination, the determination governs.** The determinations were written against a stated evidence standard; these were not.

## The snapshot gap applies here too

No page was archived at fetch time. Every URL in these files was live when fetched and its content is asserted on that basis. There is no way for a later reader to verify that a page still says what these notes record. That is the method gap registered in the citation audit, and it is at its worst here.
