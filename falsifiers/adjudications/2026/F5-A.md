**Determination.** `not_met`, on affirmative evidence that the incumbent continued in office through procedurally regular events, not on absence of contrary reporting.

---

## The adverse case, written first

### The procedural constraint on indefinite tenure has already been removed

The Thirteenth National People's Congress amended Article 79, paragraph 3 of the state constitution on 11 March 2018, deleting the two-consecutive-term limit on the President and Vice President. The vote was 2,958 in favour, 2 against, 3 abstentions ([State Council Information Office](http://english.scio.gov.cn/topnews/2018-03/12/content_50698883.htm); [Library of Congress Global Legal Monitor](https://www.loc.gov/item/global-legal-monitor/2018-05-18/china-2018-constitutional-amendment-adopted/); [NPC Observer](https://npcobserver.com/2018/03/11/china-constitution-amendment/)).

### The two offices that actually matter were never term-limited at all

Neither General Secretary of the Party nor Chairman of the Party's Central Military Commission carries a term limit in the Party Charter. The two-term practice at that level was a post-2002 **norm**, not a rule ([Congressional Research Service, via National Security Archive](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/sites/default/files/documents/r1x9o6-6lhfg/20180323%20IF10854.pdf)).

The adverse inference is direct: a system whose succession constraint is a norm rather than a rule cannot register an extra-constitutional transfer, because there is no procedure to depart from. On that reading Sub-clause A is not `not_met` but **unfalsifiable**, which is a more serious finding than a triggering one.

### No successor has been designated

There is no identified heir apparent holding the positions that previous successions used as the staging post. Combined with the removal of both military Vice Chairmen of the Central Military Commission from their posts or from the Party (see `F5-B.md`), the published scholarly reading is that no orderly path exists: "There are no durable procedures for succession. There is only the leader and the system he holds together" ([Johnson, "Late Stalinism in Beijing", China Brief 26(3), 26 Jan 2026](https://jamestown.org/late-stalinism-in-beijing/)).

### Why the adverse case does not carry

It argues that a future transfer is **unconstrained**, and that the clause may be badly specified. It does not argue that a transfer **occurred**. Sub-clause A tests an event, and the adverse case offers none.

---

## Reasoning

### Affirmative evidence that no transfer occurred

Rule 1 forbids resting `not_met` on absence of evidence. The affirmative evidence is that the incumbent exercised the functions of the paramount leadership position at scheduled, procedurally regular events across the evaluation window: presiding over the Fourth Plenum of the 20th Central Committee on 23 October 2025 ([State Council of the PRC](https://english.www.gov.cn/news/202510/23/content_WS68fa3825c6d00ca5f9a06fbe.html)), and attending the March 2026 CPPCC session, at which the reported news was the **absence of two other Politburo members**, not of the General Secretary ([Reuters, 4 Mar 2026](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/two-senior-communist-party-officials-absent-top-chinese-political-event-2026-03-04/)).

Every senior removal in the window was initiated and confirmed by civilian Party organs -- Politburo, Central Commission for Discipline Inspection, Central Committee in plenary session -- through the standing procedure. That is evidence of procedural continuity in the exercise of leadership authority, which is what this clause tests.

### The specification does the work here, and it does it against the falsifier

The adapter is explicit that amendment of term limits or succession procedures does not count, "however consequential", and that the reference point is the documents **in force at the time of the transfer**. The 2018 amendment was itself enacted by constitutional process. Under the pre-registered wording it is outside this clause.

This is the clause's designed narrowness operating as designed. Sub-clause A is a test for extra-constitutional transfer, not for norm erosion. The adapter says so in terms: "Test 8 is what stops it becoming a norm-erosion detector. Both would be interesting conditions; neither is this one."

### Coup and irregular-transfer allegations

Allegations of an irregular transfer or a loss of effective control circulated during the window. They are inadmissible for `met` under the framework's evidence standards -- anonymous or single-outlet reporting is insufficient alone -- and they are also directly contradicted. See `F5-C.md`, where the same body of reporting is assessed at length; the assessment is not duplicated here.

## Contestability

`contestable: false` on the determination. No source in the record asserts that a transfer of the paramount leadership position occurred in the evaluation window.

**The specification is contestable, and that is the finding worth carrying forward.** If the paramount position's tenure is governed by a norm rather than a procedure, Sub-clause A may be incapable of firing under any realistic scenario short of a coup -- in which case it is Sub-clause C, not A, that carries the load, and the disjunction is narrower in practice than the pre-registered wording suggests. This is recorded as a limitation of the pre-registration. **It is not repaired**, because widening a clause after observing that it is hard to satisfy is precisely the move the pre-registration exists to prevent.

**Amendment 1 narrows this paragraph. The paragraph above is left standing as written; the correction is appended below rather than substituted into it.**

## Amendments

### Amendment 1 -- 2026-08-19 -- the unfalsifiability concern is overstated

**Determination unchanged: `not_met`.** What changes is the recorded limitation.

The dry run at `../dry-run/1976.md` exercised this sub-clause against the succession following the death of Mao Zedong and returned **`met`**, on affirmative documentary evidence:

- The CPC Constitution adopted 28 August 1973 assigned the election of the Chairman of the Central Committee to the Central Committee **in plenary session**, under Article 9 ([1973 CCP Constitution](https://www.marxists.org/subject/china/documents/cpc/CONSTITUTION_CPC.htm); [Peking Review 35--36, 1973](https://www.marxists.org/subject/china/peking-review/1973/PR1973-35-36.pdf)).
- On 7 October 1976 the Politburo alone resolved that Hua Guofeng would hold the Chairmanship. The Central Committee ratified it at the Third Plenum of 16--21 July 1977, some nine months later ([Teiwes and Sun](https://www.bannedthought.net/China/MaoEra/GPCR/BourgeoisCommentary/China'sOctoberCoupThatEndedTheMaoistEra-Sun-Teiwes.pdf)).

A constitutional text naming an organ, a different organ performing the act, and a dated interval between the act and its ratification are exactly the elements the clause asks for. **Sub-clause A is not unfalsifiable. It is falsifiable, and it has been falsified against a historical case.**

The accurate limitation is narrower and is substituted for the earlier one:

> Sub-clause A is falsifiable in principle. It is hard to satisfy under the post-1982 constitutional arrangement specifically, because the Party Chairmanship -- the office whose filling the 1973 text governed by explicit procedure -- was abolished, and the offices that now carry paramount authority have been filled by the procedures that govern them. The clause is quiet because the procedure has held. That is a substantive finding about the object of study and not a defect in the instrument.

The operative half of the earlier concern survives: **Sub-clause C, not A, carries the practical load** in a system where the succession constraint at the top is a norm rather than a rule. The 1976 exercise supports that too, since C also returned `met` there. What does not survive is the stronger claim that A cannot fire at all.

**Recording this cuts against the project's interest and is recorded for that reason.** An unfalsifiable clause is a more striking methodological finding than a clause that is merely quiet, and the earlier wording was the more publishable of the two. It was also wrong, and the correction is appended rather than substituted into the original paragraph.
