# F8 dry run -- 2021 expected-negative, 2025 expected-positive

**Nothing in this file is a finding about the present**, and per the committed flag-only scope for F8 (fix window shut at ~1.1pp), nothing here changes any rule or live determination. Findings are carried as flags.

**Outcome, stated at the head: the share arithmetic discriminates -- F8 is the only near-threshold condition whose measurand visits both sides of its threshold within the observed record.** On the public tracked-cluster construction, the PRC/US ratio stands at 109.6 percent in 2021 (condition not satisfied, correctly) and 17.3 percent in 2025 (condition satisfied, correctly). One sharp flag: the spread between admissible constructions (~1.7 points, against the adapter's own ~19 percent proxy) **exceeds** the live distance to threshold (~1.0 point).

## The adverse case, written first

**Against the expected-negative's known answer.** The 2019--2021 ratios near 100 percent are inflated by a documented coverage asymmetry: the dataset's early years are dominated by publicly reported Chinese and academic HPC systems, while US private and cloud clusters of that era are under-tracked. The true 2021 ratio was plausibly far lower. **Response, with the margin argument:** the verdict tolerates enormous overstatement. For the 2021 expected-negative to flip to satisfied, the tracked ratio of 109.6 percent would have to overstate the truth by more than a factor of five. The coverage bias is real, recorded as a deficiency, and cannot plausibly be that large while the same era's public record carried PRC systems at the top of global rankings.

**Against the exercise's usefulness.** The construction here is one of several admissible under the adapter, and its 2025 value (17.3 percent) differs from the proxy the adapter itself carries (~19 percent). If constructions disagree by more than the distance to threshold, discriminating at 110-versus-17 says little about resolving 19-versus-20. Conceded -- and converted into the exercise's sharpest flag rather than deflected.

## Findings

### Finding 1 -- discrimination: pass, on a construction that visits both sides

Unlike F6 (threshold never reached) and F2 (threshold never left), F8's measurand crosses its threshold inside the observed record, and the arithmetic classifies both sides correctly with wide margins. Among the four near-threshold-or-untestable conditions examined today, F8 is the only one whose discrimination could actually be tested -- and it passed.

### Finding 2 -- `construction_spread_exceeds_margin`

The construction computed here gives 17.3 percent for 2025; the proxy carried in the adapter's own current-position note implies roughly 19 percent. The spread between admissible constructions of the same concept (~1.7 points on these two constructions alone -- 19 minus 17.3; the full mandated sensitivity grid is wider) exceeds the ~1.0-point live distance from the adapter's proxy to the threshold. **Any near-threshold F8 verdict is therefore construction-determined until the committed construction is fully pinned** -- which is exactly what the adapter's unresolved method items (precision basis, intermediated-access band) leave open. Those items remain out of scope per the committed Q&A scope; this flag quantifies why they are the binding gap. One-directional statement: the flag blocks confident resolution near the threshold in either direction; it does not favour a side.

### Finding 3 -- the persistence clock is construction-dependent

"Remains below 20 percent" requires a start date for *remains*. On this construction the condition first holds in 2025; on the adapter's proxy it already held earlier. A persistence requirement whose clock start moves by years across admissible constructions inherits Finding 2's problem in time as well as level. Recorded for the eventual first ingest.

## Evidentiary deficiencies

1. Reconnaissance preceded registration, disclosed at the head.
2. Single dataset, single construction; the mandated sensitivity grid (factor-of-four boundary, alternative attribution) was not run.
3. Early-year coverage asymmetry, argued immaterial to the 2021 verdict only by the factor-of-five margin.
4. Cumulative-stock construction ignores depreciation beyond recorded decommission dates.
