## Why provisional

F5 is Type C. Its specification requires each of three sub-clauses to be separately adjudicated, each with a written adverse case, named sources, and a dissent record.

**No formal adjudication has been authored.** This entry therefore does not meet F5's own evidentiary standard and is marked provisional rather than presented as a determination.

Recording it as a completed `not_triggered` would be the more comfortable option and would also be the first breach of the framework. The deficiency is stated instead.
