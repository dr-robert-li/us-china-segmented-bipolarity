# F5 -- 2026

**Condition.** PRC succession fails destructively.
**Threshold.** Extra-constitutional leadership transfer, a purge exceeding 15% of the Politburo, or military intervention in succession, by 2040.
**Verdict.** `not_triggered` -- **provisional**

## Why provisional

F5 is Type C. Its specification requires each of three sub-clauses to be separately adjudicated, each with a written adverse case, named sources, and a dissent record.

**No formal adjudication has been authored.** This entry therefore does not meet F5's own evidentiary standard and is marked provisional rather than presented as a determination.

Recording it as a completed `not_triggered` would be the more comfortable option and would also be the first breach of the framework. The deficiency is stated instead.

## Provisional per-sub-clause position

| Sub-clause | Provisional position | Adjudication authored |
|---|---|---|
| A -- extra-constitutional transfer | No qualifying event identified | No |
| B -- purge exceeding 15% of Politburo | No qualifying removal cluster identified | No |
| C -- military intervention in succession | No qualifying event identified | No |

## Sub-clause B arithmetic

The threshold is `ceil(0.15 * seated_politburo_count)`, computed from the seated count recorded from primary Party sources at the start of the evaluation year.

**The seated count has not been recorded from primary sources for this entry.** Until it is, the member threshold is undefined and Sub-clause B cannot be evaluated even provisionally on the arithmetic.

For orientation only, and not as a recorded value: a Politburo seated at 24 full members would yield a threshold of 4 members.

## Deficiencies to close

1. Record the seated Politburo count from primary Party sources.
2. Author the three sub-clause adjudications with adverse cases.
3. Publish the dry-run adjudication against a historical episode, labelled unmistakably as a rubric exercise.

`preliminary`

2027, at which point this entry should be superseded by a compliant determination. If it is not, the papers should note that the only pure Type C condition has never been formally adjudicated.

### Seated count, now recorded from primary sources

Deficiency 1 is closed. The 20th Politburo comprises **24 full members including the 7-member Standing Committee**, elected 22--23 October 2022 ([International Department of the CPC Central Committee](https://www.idcpc.org.cn/english2023/tjzl/cpcjj/leadershipof20thCentralCommittee/)), corroborated by [CSIS](https://csis-website-prod.s3.amazonaws.com/s3fs-public/2023-04/230427_China_20th_Politburo_0.pdf).

### F5-B is now the live limb of the whole set

At one to two members, Sub-clause B is arguably nearer its threshold than any other condition. **A single further officially announced expulsion of a full Politburo member inside the counting window satisfies it.** F5 should be reviewed on announcement rather than annually.

This changes the calibration statement carried in the README and the papers. The two nearest conditions were recorded as F2 (~0.1pp) and F8 (~1.1pp), both measured indirectly. F5-B now joins them, and F5 has **no series at all**. More of the set's proximity to falsification sits on adjudicated and definitional ground than the threshold table showed.

**This was the last moment at which that fix was uncontaminated.** After one further removal, either resolution becomes outcome-determining, and a fix made then would be indistinguishable from fitting the rule to the result. The fix does not change the 2026 determination under either reading.

## Amendment 2 -- 2026-08-19 -- dry-run exercise; Sub-clause B false-negative finding

**The 2026 determination is unchanged: `not_triggered`.** What changes is how much weight that determination can bear.

### What was done

Deficiency 3 required a dry run against a closed historical episode. Two were published, paired by design so that the exercise tests discrimination rather than sensitivity:

| Exercise | Episode | Role |
|---|---|---|
| [`dry-run/1976.md`](../../adjudications/dry-run/1976.md) | Succession after the death of Mao Zedong | Expected-positive |
| [`dry-run/2012.md`](../../adjudications/dry-run/2012.md) | Hu Jintao to Xi Jinping transition | Negative control |

Failure conditions were named before either was computed: the rubric fails if it returns the negative verdict on the positive episode, the positive verdict on the control, or the same verdict on both.

### Result

| Sub-clause | 1976 | 2012 | Discriminates? |
|---|---|---|---|
| A -- extra-constitutional transfer | `met` | `not_met` | Yes |
| B -- purge above 15 percent | **`not_met`** | `not_met` | **No** |
| C -- military intervention | `met` | `not_met` | Yes |

**Sub-clause B failed.** It returns `not_met` on the archetypal case of an extra-constitutional seizure of power in the record, triggering two of the three pre-named failure conditions at once.

### The mechanism, and why it is worse than a mis-set threshold

The 1976 denominator is 21 full members, giving `ceil(0.15 * 21) = 4`. The window 1 January 1975 to 31 December 1976 contains, under the adapter's committed anchoring -- first official public announcement of expulsion -- exactly **one** qualifying removal. The Gang of Four were arrested on 6 October 1976 but expelled only at the Third Plenum of 16--21 July 1977, outside the window.

Two alternative anchorings both return `met`: effective loss of office by arrest or published dismissal gives 5, and seats vacated by any cause including death gives 10.

The defect is therefore not that the 15 percent threshold sits at the wrong level. **It is that the anchoring rule is anti-correlated with the property the clause measures.** An orderly, procedurally regular removal is announced promptly and counts. An extra-constitutional seizure is announced late, or not at all, and does not. Sub-clause B is most likely to miss precisely the events it exists to detect.

Amendment 1 to `pipeline/adapters/F5.md`, authored earlier the same day, adopted this anchoring with reasons recorded and noted that it made the clause harder to satisfy. Those reasons -- dated, public, primary, uniform, analyst-independent -- remain sound as auditability criteria. **The dry run shows they were the wrong criteria to optimise for.** An anchoring rule chosen for auditability was allowed to determine what the clause could see.

### Why the rule was not fixed

The better rule -- effective loss of office -- would move the live 2026 determination toward triggering. Zhang Youxia, reported detained in January 2026, would count under effective-loss anchoring, taking the numerator from 2 to 3 against a threshold of 4.

Adopting it now is prohibited. The pre-registration forbids changing a rule as its threshold is approached, and the prohibition is **direction-neutral**: a change that makes the project's own thesis easier to support is not more acceptable than one that makes it harder. The change would be substantively correct and procedurally indefensible, and the second consideration governs.

A candidate replacement anchoring is registered in `pipeline/adapters/F5.md` by Amendment 3, to take effect **prospectively from evaluation year 2027 only**.

### Consequences for the 2026 record

1. **F5 Sub-clause B's `not_triggered` carries a known false-negative mode.** It should not be read as evidence that no purge-scale event occurred, only that none was registered by a counting rule now demonstrated to be insensitive to the class of event at issue.
2. **The baseline log's calibration statement is strengthened, not weakened.** The log already recorded that the two conditions nearest their thresholds are the two whose measurement is least direct. Sub-clause B's measurement is now shown to be less direct than that statement assumed.
3. **Excluding deaths from the numerator is load-bearing, not technical.** Five of 21 seats fell vacant by death inside the 1976 window -- 24 percent, above the threshold on its own. The exclusion is correct: death in office is not a purge. But it means the clause's arithmetic is sensitive to a definitional choice of the same magnitude as the threshold itself.

### Sub-clause A -- the earlier limitation is overstated and is narrowed

The limitation recorded above held that Sub-clause A "may be close to unfalsifiable" because the paramount position's tenure is governed by a norm rather than a procedure. The 1976 exercise falsifies that reading. The 1973 Party Constitution assigned the election of the Chairman to a plenary session under Article 9; in October 1976 the Politburo alone appointed Hua Guofeng, and a plenary session ratified it nine months later. **The clause fires cleanly on affirmative documentary evidence.**

The accurate statement is narrower: Sub-clause A is falsifiable in principle and hard to satisfy under the post-1982 arrangement specifically, because the Party Chairmanship was abolished and the offices that now carry paramount authority are filled by procedures that have been followed. The clause is not empty. It is quiet because the procedure has held, which is a substantive finding and not a defect in the instrument. Amended in `../../adjudications/2026/F5-A.md`.

### What the negative control establishes

Sub-clause B returns `not_met` on 2012 under **every** anchoring considered, including the two that flip 1976. The rubric can be made to miss a purge by choice of anchoring; it cannot be made to invent one. The defect is one-directional, which bounds it: F5-B's false-positive risk is low and its false-negative risk is demonstrated.

### Standing of these exercises

Neither dry run is a determination. Neither binds any live verdict. Neither is a finding about the present. They are excluded from the annual index and filed under `../../adjudications/dry-run/`, with conventions in that directory's README.
