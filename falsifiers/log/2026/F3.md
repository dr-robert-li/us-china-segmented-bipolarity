**Distance on outturn.** Approximately 63 percentage points.

**Distance on the projection path.** Approximately 17 percentage points at 2034, the last projected year before the deadline. The condition is not projected to be satisfied on the current baseline, but not by a wide margin at the end of the horizon.

Both are reported because reporting only the outturn distance would understate the trajectory, and reporting only the projection would breach the outturn-only rule.

## Cross-check

An alternative augmented construction places the 2024 figure nearer 124% of GDP, against the committed 117.0%. The spread of roughly seven percentage points is **within** the 10-point tolerance and does not raise `source_disagreement`. It is recorded because it demonstrates that "augmented" is not a single standardised construction.

Context, not feeding the verdict: augmented fiscal deficit of approximately 12.5-13.2% of GDP for 2024 against a headline general-government deficit near 3.1%.
