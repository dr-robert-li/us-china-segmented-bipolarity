It is recorded because it demonstrates that "augmented" is not a single standardised construction.

Context, not feeding the verdict: augmented fiscal deficit of approximately 12.5-13.2% of GDP for 2024 against a headline general-government deficit near 3.1%.

## Amendment 01 -- vintage revision, recorded 2026-08-19

Appended, not overwritten, per the logging rule in `falsifiers/PRE-REGISTRATION.md`. The original entry above stands as the record of what was known at the time it was written.

**Trigger.** A newer IMF Article IV vintage supersedes the series the original entry used. Under `pipeline/schema.md` design rule 1, vintage is a first-class field and two observations of the same quantity from different vintages are two records, not one record updated. This amendment creates the second record.

| Quantity | Original entry | 2025 Article IV vintage | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Augmented debt, 2023 | -- | 126.6% of GDP | reported |
| Augmented debt, 2024 | 117.0% | 135.3% of GDP | reported, superseding |
| Augmented debt, 2025 | -- | 141.5% of GDP | reported |
| Augmented debt, 2026 | -- | 146.2% of GDP | projected, cannot trigger |
| Later projection path | ~150.0%, ~162.7% | 150.0%, then 153.7% | projected, cannot trigger |

Source: [IMF, People's Republic of China: 2025 Article IV Consultation, Country Report No. 2026/001](https://www.imf.org/-/media/files/publications/cr/2026/english/1chnea2026001-source-pdf.pdf).

**Revised verdict.** `not_triggered`. Unchanged.

**Revised distance on outturn.** Approximately 38.5 percentage points, against 180%, on the 2025 outturn of 141.5%. The original entry recorded approximately 63 points against a 2024 outturn of 117.0%.

**What the revision means.** The 2024 figure was revised upward by roughly 18 percentage points between vintages. That is a large revision on a series feeding a pre-registered threshold, and it is larger than the roughly 7-point spread between augmented constructions that the original entry recorded as a cross-check. The construction spread was within tolerance; the vintage revision is not the same kind of object and is not covered by that tolerance.

Two consequences follow, and neither is a threshold change.

1. The `source_disagreement` tolerance of 10 points in `pipeline/adapters/F3.md` was calibrated against construction spread, not against vintage revision. A revision of this size would not have raised the flag under the current rule because it is a supersession rather than a disagreement between contemporaneous sources. **Recorded as a specification gap**, to be addressed in the adapter by a separate rule for vintage-revision magnitude. The threshold is not adjusted here, and the gap is not repaired in the same commit that discovered it.
2. The distance to threshold has closed by roughly 24 points in one revision cycle without any change in the underlying fiscal position. The papers must report the outturn distance alongside its vintage, because "63 points away" and "38.5 points away" are both true statements about the same condition at different publication dates.

**Clause 2.** Still not adjudicated. The 160% rubric-exercise trigger has not been reached on outturn, though it is now approximately 18.5 points away rather than 43. Clause 2 is expected to require a published dry-run adjudication within the next two to three review cycles on the current projection path.

**Cross-check, not feeding the verdict.** An independent flow-based estimate places the overall government deficit at 9.0% of GDP for full-year 2025, with possible widening to 9.5-10.0% in 2026 ([Rhodium Group](https://rhg.com/research/chinas-financial-and-fiscal-decay/)). This is a deficit flow on a different methodology and is not comparable to the augmented debt stock above. It is recorded because it is directionally consistent, and flagged as non-comparable so that it cannot later be cited as corroboration of the stock figure. China's official headline deficit widened to 4% of GDP in 2025 from 3% in 2024, per the same Article IV.

**Flags raised.** `vintage_revision_large`.
