# F8 -- 2026

**Condition.** Frontier compute gap persists decisively.
**Threshold.** PRC aggregate frontier-capable compute remains below 20% of the US total, through 2032.
**Verdict.** `indeterminate`

## The position, stated carefully

On the best available public proxy the PRC/US ratio is **below** the threshold, meaning the condition is currently **satisfied** on that proxy.

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| PRC share of global AI supercomputer performance | ~14.1% |
| US share of same | ~74.5% |
| Implied PRC/US ratio | **~18.9%** |
| Threshold | 20% |
| Distance | ~1.1pp below |

F8 asks whether the ratio **remains** below 20% through 2032. The operative question is persistence, not attainment. An early `triggered` verdict is the prior expectation rather than a surprise, and must not be reported as an alarming signal.

## Why the verdict is nonetheless indeterminate

Three reasons, any one sufficient:

1. The proxy measures AI supercomputer performance shares, **not** frontier-capable installed stock on the committed definition.
2. It is a **share-of-global** construction converted to a bilateral ratio. The committed construction is bilateral directly.
3. No uncertainty **band** has been established. The verdict rule requires the band to lie wholly on one side of 20%, and with the point estimate 1.1 percentage points below the threshold, almost any plausible band straddles it.

Point 3 alone is decisive. A condition this close to its boundary, measured this indirectly, should not return a confident verdict.

## Progress since specification

The declared precision basis is now committed as dense BF16/FP16 tensor throughput, resolving the first of four open items and enabling rule R004.

The factor-of-eight window at current values implies a floor near 281 TFLOPS dense BF16, which admits prior-generation flagship hardware around 312 TFLOPS. The window is therefore wider than the boundary file's stated justification of two generations. The factor is unchanged; the description is corrected.

## Remaining blockers to a published verdict

- Shipment and utilisation estimation method, with error characterisation
- Whether a bilateral construction exists in published sources
- The intermediated-access band, still with no committed estimation method

F8 may emit provisional monitoring output, clearly labelled, but no published verdict until the first is resolved.
