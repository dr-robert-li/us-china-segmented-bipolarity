# F7 -- 2026

**Condition.** PRC robotics fails to offset labour contraction.
**Threshold.** Manufacturing TFP growth negative for five consecutive years despite continued IFR-recorded robot-density growth, by 2040.
**Verdict.** `indeterminate`

## Why indeterminate

The condition specifies **manufacturing** TFP. The named source publishes total-economy productivity and does not provide sector-level TFP for the PRC at the required granularity.

Under the committed rule, total-economy TFP may be used as a **declared proxy** but **no `triggered` verdict may be emitted on proxy data**. The strongest available verdict is therefore `indeterminate`, and this holds irrespective of what the proxy shows.

**Resolution condition.** Identification of a defensible manufacturing-specific TFP series for the PRC, committed by diff before any evaluation year in which it would be load-bearing.

## What has not been computed

Neither clause has been evaluated for any year. In particular the **density decomposition** has not been run:

```
d ln(D) = d ln(stock) - d ln(employment)
```

This is the substantive content of the adapter. Robot density rises when manufacturing employment falls, so density growth could be satisfied by labour contraction with zero installations -- which would invert the condition's meaning. Until the decomposition is computed, nothing useful can be said about the robotics clause.

## Deficiencies to close

1. Identify a manufacturing-specific TFP series, or record formally that none exists.
2. Establish the manufacturing employment series and its definitional boundary, including migrant and informal employment.
3. Establish the operational robot-stock methodology, including assumed service life, since stock is modelled rather than counted.

## Standing caveat

If item 1 cannot be resolved, F7 can only ever return `not_triggered` or `indeterminate`. A falsifier that cannot fire is a weakened falsifier, and the papers must state this rather than reporting F7 alongside conditions that can resolve.
