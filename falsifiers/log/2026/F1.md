# F1 -- 2026

**Condition.** US generation-capacity additions converge on China's.
**Threshold.** 40% of PRC annual additions, three-year rolling average, by 2035.
**Verdict.** `not_triggered`

## Observed

| Year | US additions (GW) | PRC additions (GW) | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2024 | 48.6 | ~429 | ~11.3% |
| 2025 | 53 | ~540 | ~9.8% |

No three-year rolling average is defined on two observations, so the committed construction (ratio of three-year sums) is not yet computable. The annual ratios are reported instead.

**Distance to threshold.** Approximately 30 percentage points on the 2025 annual ratio.

## Direction

The ratio moved **away** from the threshold between 2024 and 2025, despite 2025 being reported as the largest single-year US capacity addition since 2002. Denominator growth outpaced numerator growth.

## Basis and caveats

- Figures are from secondary reporting, not from the committed primary source. Re-derivation at first ingest will supersede them.
- Additions are as reported rather than differenced from year-end totals, so the committed net-of-retirements construction has not been applied.
- PRC totals cross-check against year-end installed capacity of approximately 3.35 billion kW at end-2024 and 4.01 billion kW at end-May 2026.
- No dispatchable adjustment applied, and none required: F1 is explicitly exempt.

## Flags

`basis_mixed`, `preliminary`
