# F8 adapter specification

**Condition.** Frontier compute gap persists decisively.

**Threshold.** PRC aggregate frontier-capable compute remains below 20 percent of the US total.

**Deadline.** 2032.

**Type.** A/D hybrid. See reclassification note below.

**Bears on.** **P2** primarily -- the mechanism claim that the AI-robotics transition disproportionately rewards the dimensions on which China is gaining. P3 secondarily. Maps to Pillar D, which is load-bearing, so a crossing engages the pillar-concentration gate in `falsifiers/dependence.md`.

**Depends on.** `pipeline/definitions/frontier-compute.md` for the boundary, precision basis, jurisdictional attribution, and uncertainty band. `pipeline/adjudication.md` for sustained-condition semantics.

---

## Reclassification note

`falsifiers/PRE-REGISTRATION.md` classifies F8 as Type A, direct quantitative and fully automatable. On closer specification that classification is **too generous** and is amended here to a Type A/D hybrid: the arithmetic is automatable, but the boundary definition, precision basis, and jurisdictional attribution are all methodology-sensitive judgements, exactly the property that defines Type D.

The consequence is that Type D obligations now attach: coding rules fixed in advance, and sensitivity of the verdict to those rules reported alongside the verdict.

This amendment requires justification, because reclassification is a listed prohibition. The prohibition is **directional**. It forbids reclassifying a condition in a way that *reduces* scrutiny -- moving from automated to adjudicated to escape an unwelcome computed verdict. Moving from Type A to Type A/D *increases* scrutiny by adding mandatory sensitivity reporting to a condition that would otherwise emit a bare number.

Recorded here so that the direction of the change is visible and can be checked. A future reclassification in the loosening direction would not be permissible on this reasoning.

## Why the verdict logic is inverted relative to F1

F1 triggers if a gap **closes**. F8 triggers if a gap **persists**. Two consequences follow that are easy to get wrong in implementation.

First, F8's condition is currently **satisfied** on the available proxy. The implied PRC/US ratio sits near 19 percent against a 20 percent threshold. F8 is therefore not a distant hypothetical; the operative question is persistence through 2032, not attainment.

Second, a triggered or indeterminate F8 verdict in early evaluation years is the **expected** outcome rather than a surprise, and must not be treated as an alarming signal in itself. What matters is whether the ratio crosses and stays above 20 percent before 2032, which would clear the condition, or remains below it, which would trigger.

An implementation that reports "F8 triggered" in 2027 without noting that this was the prior expectation would misinform its reader.

---

## Persistence semantics

F8 is a sustained condition, notwithstanding its Type A origins. The word "remains" carries the requirement.

Applying `pipeline/adjudication.md`:

- Per-year qualification: the year qualifies if the committed band lies wholly **below** 20 percent.
- The run is **recomputed from scratch** at every evaluation. No stateful counter.
- A year whose band straddles the threshold is `indeterminate` for that year. It does not advance the run and does not reset it.
- A single year in which the band lies wholly **at or above** 20 percent **breaks** the condition permanently. F8 is a persistence claim; one clean refutation ends it.
- Evaluation runs from the first year with admissible data through 2032 inclusive.

The permanence of the break is a deliberate asymmetry and is pre-registered as such. If PRC frontier-capable compute reaches 20 percent of the US total at any point before 2032, the claim that the gap persists decisively is false, and a subsequent fall back below the threshold does not restore it.

## Derivation

### Step 1 -- Apply the boundary

Filter installed accelerator stock to frontier-capable devices per the rolling factor-of-eight rule, on the declared dense precision basis. Excluded devices are logged, not silently dropped.

### Step 2 -- Attribute jurisdictionally

Record physical location and operator nationality for every observation. Construct the headline aggregate on legal control. Compute the offshore and intermediated-access adjustment as a **band** on the PRC figure.

### Step 3 -- Bilateral ratio

```
Ratio_t = Compute_PRC,t / Compute_US,t
```

Bilateral, not share-of-global. Where only share-of-global figures are available, the conversion is documented and the resulting record carries `basis: estimated`.

### Step 4 -- Band assembly

The reported band combines shipment and utilisation uncertainty, obsolescence treatment within the rolling window, precision-conversion assumptions, the intermediated-access adjustment, and the divergence between Constructions A and B. The band is not symmetric and is not assumed to be.

### Step 5 -- Verdict

```
year qualifies       if band_upper  <  0.20
year breaks F8       if band_lower >= 0.20
year indeterminate   otherwise
```

## Mandatory sensitivity reporting

Type D obligations. Every verdict is reported at:

1. Committed rolling factor of **eight**
2. Factor of **four** -- narrower, roughly one generation
3. Factor of **sixteen** -- wider, roughly three generations
4. Construction A instead of Construction B
5. Zero intermediated-access adjustment, as a lower bound on the PRC figure

A verdict that holds across all five is reported as a verdict. A verdict that flips is reported as `indeterminate` with the flip condition named.

Setting 5 exists because the intermediated-access adjustment is the least defensible quantity in the whole construction, and a reader is entitled to see the verdict with it switched off entirely.

## Tests

1. **Boundary application.** A device just inside and just outside the factor-of-eight window is classified correctly, and exclusions are logged.
2. **Precision conversion.** A figure published at low precision with sparsity converts to the declared dense basis, and an unconvertible device is excluded with a log entry.
3. **Official-aggregate exclusion.** PRC aggregate compute figures supplied to the ratio must fail validation, not be silently accepted.
4. **Symmetric exclusion.** A mixed US aggregate is rejected on the same rule.
5. **Break permanence.** A year with `band_lower >= 0.20` permanently ends the condition; subsequent qualifying years do not restore it.
6. **Straddle handling.** A band spanning 0.20 yields `indeterminate`, not a point verdict.
7. **Sensitivity divergence.** Where the five sensitivity settings disagree on the verdict, output is `indeterminate` with the flip condition named.
8. **Recompute.** A revision to an early-year estimate correctly shortens or lengthens the recomputed run.

## Open items

Inherited from the boundary definition and unresolved:

- Declared precision basis not yet selected.
- Shipment and utilisation estimation method not yet specified, nor its error characterisation.
- No committed estimation method for the intermediated-access band. This is the binding gap; until it exists, F8 can only report with setting 5 as its lower bound.
- Whether a defensible bilateral construction exists in published sources, or whether share-of-global conversion is unavoidable.

F8 should not emit a published verdict until the first two are resolved. It may emit provisional monitoring output, clearly labelled, in the interim.
