# F3 adapter specification

**Condition.** PRC debt forces disorderly adjustment.

**Threshold.** IMF augmented debt exceeds 180 percent of GDP **with** an unmanaged LGFV default cascade.

**Deadline.** 2035.

**Type.** B -- compound. Quantitative clause automates; qualitative clause is adjudicated.

**Bears on.** P1 only. **Consistent with P3.** A crossing shifts posterior mass toward the peaking-power reading, which is nested as a parameter region rather than opposed, so F3 must not be scored as refuting segmented bipolarity.

**Depends on.** `pipeline/adjudication.md` for the qualitative clause and the compound-assembly table.

**Correlated with.** F7 at 0.45, the highest cross-condition correlation in the matrix and the pair that motivated amending the original two-falsifier rejection rule.

## Clause 1 -- Augmented debt above 180 percent of GDP

### Committed series

IMF Article IV **augmented debt**, which includes local government financing vehicle liabilities and off-budget government funds. Headline general-government debt is inadmissible, per `DATA-INTEGRITY.md`.

### The definitional-variance problem

"Augmented" is not a single standardised construction. For the 2024 reference year, the IMF Article IV figure is approximately **117.0 percent** of GDP, while an independent analysis using a similar augmented framework puts it nearer **124 percent**, up from 86.3 percent in 2019. That is a spread of roughly seven percentage points on the same nominal concept.

A related but distinct measure, the augmented fiscal **deficit**, has been reported at approximately 12.5 to 13.2 percent of GDP for 2024 against a headline general-government deficit near 3.1 percent. The gap between augmented and headline framings is therefore large in both stock and flow terms, which is precisely why the augmented framing was mandated.

**Committed rule.** The IMF Article IV series is the sole input to the verdict. Alternative augmented constructions are ingested as cross-checks and raise `source_disagreement` beyond a tolerance of **10 percentage points** of GDP. They are never averaged with the IMF series. Averaging two differently-constructed measures produces a number that measures neither.

### Outturn, not projection

**This is the most important rule in the adapter.**

IMF Article IV publications contain both outturn estimates for elapsed years and projections for future years. Current projections place augmented debt near **150 percent** of GDP by 2029 and roughly **162.7 percent** by 2034 -- below the 180 percent threshold but not by a wide margin at the end of the horizon.

The threshold is tested **only on outturn**. A projection crossing 180 percent does **not** satisfy Clause 1, in any vintage, for any year.

Without this rule, a single unfavourable forecast revision could fire a pre-registered falsifier without anything having actually happened. Projections are ingested and tracked, because their trajectory is informative, but they are recorded with `basis: estimated` and are structurally incapable of triggering.

### Distance tracking

Because outturn governs, the annual log records three quantities: the latest outturn value, the distance from outturn to 180 percent, and the latest projection path with its vintage. A reader can then see both what has happened and what is expected, without the two being conflated.

## Clause 2 -- Unmanaged LGFV default cascade

Adjudicated under `pipeline/adjudication.md`. The rubric below supplements that framework; it does not replace any of its rules, and the adverse-interest requirement applies in full.

### Where the burden sits

The operative word is **unmanaged**, not *default* and not *cascade*. Chinese local-government debt has been subject to repeated large-scale managed restructuring, refinancing, and maturity extension. None of that satisfies this clause. A managed workout, even a very large one, is evidence of capacity to absorb stress rather than of disorderly adjustment.

The clause therefore requires evidence that authorities **attempted and failed** to contain a default sequence, or **declined to intervene** and contagion followed.

### Indicative criteria

No single criterion is sufficient. The determination is holistic and must be reasoned in prose, but the following are the facts the adjudication must address explicitly, one by one:

| Criterion | What counts toward `met` |
|---|---|
| **Scale** | Multiple distinct LGFV issuers, across more than one province, in a bounded window |
| **Instrument seniority** | Missed payments on **public bonds**, not solely on non-standard or private-placement instruments |
| **Intervention** | Central or provincial authorities either failed to prevent the sequence after acting, or declined to act |
| **Contagion** | Spread beyond LGFVs -- to provincial issuance costs, bank asset quality, or unrelated credit markets |
| **Disorder** | Losses imposed without an orderly framework; disputed priority; abrupt market closure to a class of issuers |
| **Persistence** | Sequence not resolved within the same fiscal year by a workout |

### What does not count

- Technical or curable payment delays
- Non-standard product defaults absent public-bond defaults
- Pre-announced restructuring or refinancing programmes, at any scale
- Single-issuer distress, however large
- Rating actions, spread widening, or analyst commentary in the absence of missed payments

The last exclusion matters. Much of the available reporting on this topic is anticipatory rather than factual, and under the evidence standards it is insufficient for `met` on its own.

### Evidence handling

Official PRC statements are admissible as evidence of a **position** -- for instance, that authorities consider a situation contained -- never as evidence of the underlying fact. Single-outlet or anonymously-sourced reporting is insufficient alone for `met`. Both rules are inherited from the adjudication framework and are restated here because this is the clause where they bind hardest.

## Compound assembly

### Contemporaneity

**Both clauses must be satisfied in the same evaluation year.**

This is pre-committed because the condition's wording is conjunctive: debt exceeding the threshold *with* a cascade. A cascade in 2028 followed by a debt crossing in 2034 would not describe a single episode of disorderly adjustment, and counting it would convert a conjunctive condition into a disjunctive one by accumulation.

### Assembly

Per the table in `pipeline/adjudication.md`. Note that a quantitative `not_met` short-circuits: while outturn augmented debt remains below 180 percent, F3 cannot trigger regardless of credit conditions, and **no adjudication of Clause 2 is required**.

Clause 2 is nonetheless monitored and adjudicated once outturn augmented debt exceeds **160 percent** of GDP, so that the rubric is exercised before it is load-bearing. Adjudicating a clause for the first time in the year it determines a verdict would be poor practice.

## Tests

1. **Projection cannot trigger.** A projection series value of 185 percent must produce `not_met`, not `met`.
2. **Vintage separation.** Outturn and projection records for the same year from different vintages are distinguishable and do not merge.
3. **Averaging prohibition.** Supplying two augmented constructions must not produce an averaged value; it must produce a cross-check comparison.
4. **Tolerance.** A 12 percentage-point IMF-versus-alternative divergence raises `source_disagreement`.
5. **Headline rejection.** A headline general-government debt figure supplied to Clause 1 fails validation.
6. **Short-circuit.** With outturn below 180 percent, the verdict is `not_triggered` and no adjudication record is required.
7. **Rubric exercise trigger.** Outturn crossing 160 percent requires a Clause 2 adjudication even though the verdict is `not_triggered`.
8. **Contemporaneity.** Clause 2 `met` in year t and Clause 1 `met` in year t+3 must not assemble to `triggered`.
9. **Adverse case.** An adjudication record with an empty `adverse_case` fails validation.

Test 1 is the one that protects the condition from its own data source. Test 8 protects it from accumulation.

None of the three pre-named failure conditions occurred.

**Scope of the validation, restated from the registration:** generic cascade discrimination only. Nothing LGFV-specific is validated, Clause 1's arithmetic was not exercised, and the compound-assembly rules remain covered by synthetic tests only.
